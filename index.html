Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 | Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to NewsBite (referred to as "NewsBite", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to transparency, user autonomy, and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process, how data is handled across our news aggregation and community reporting services, and how we strictly comply with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

📰

1. Service Overview & Purpose

NewsBite provides real-time curated global journalism across technology, science, business, world affairs, and culture. The app features:

  • Public News Feed: Breaking news articles curated from verified global feeds and AI-assisted summaries powered by Gemini.
  • Community News Desk: An optional eyewitness submission portal allowing readers to contribute breaking stories, local event updates, and citizen reports to the editorial team.
  • Encrypted Contributor Communications: Secure end-to-end authenticated channels allowing verified journalists and contributors to correspond with editorial desks without unencrypted third-party surveillance.
📱

2. Device Permissions & Transparent Disclosure

In strict accordance with Google Play's User Data & Prominent Disclosure Policies, NewsBite accesses device hardware features only when triggered by direct user interaction in specific user-facing capabilities:

📷 Camera (CAMERA) Allows you to take eyewitness photos to attach to breaking news stories in the Community News Desk, or capture a profile avatar. Never accessed in the background.
🖼️ Media Files (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) Enables you to select existing pictures or press materials from your device photo gallery to submit alongside community news reports.
🎙️ Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Allows you to record on-the-scene eyewitness voice notes and audio reports to attach to breaking news stories in the Community News Desk. The microphone is accessed only when you actively tap the voice recording button.
🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Sends critical breaking news headlines, curated editorial updates, and community discussion reply notices. Can be customized or toggled off at any time in system settings.
🌐 Network Access (INTERNET) Used to retrieve the latest real-time news articles from verified public RSS and news providers, and synchronize community discussion boards.
🛡️

3. Data Protection & Security Architecture

Our backend infrastructure is engineered with industry-standard encryption standards:

  • Transport Security: All client-server traffic is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (HTTPS / Secure WebSockets).
  • Encrypted Communications: Real-time peer audio/video calls use WebRTC with Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-time Transport Protocol (SRTP).
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal user information, reading history, or contact data to third-party ad networks or data brokers.
🗂️

4. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliance)

You maintain complete ownership and control over your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play User Data policies:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated profile records directly within the app at any time by going to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately.
  • Web-Based Account Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, you can submit an account and personal data deletion request by emailing sidaka36@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address.
  • Data Retention & Purge Timeline: Upon receiving an account deletion request, all personal identifiers (email, username, avatar), conversation history, and uploaded news media are permanently purged from our servers within 14 calendar days. No residual telemetry or backup data is retained.
  • Local Cache Clearing: You can purge all cached articles, bookmarks, and temporary data at any time via Settings > Clear Application Cache.
👶

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

NewsBite is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.
